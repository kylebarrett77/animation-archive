## Priority Recommendations for Studio Deep-Dives

### 1. TOEI ANIMATION — 1970s Super Robot Block (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
**Why:** Mazinger Z, Grendizer, Captain Harlock (original TV), and the Voltron source shows (Golion/Dairugger) represent some of the most culturally significant sci-fi anime ever made. Grendizer alone reshaped pop culture across the entire Middle East and Europe. These are exactly the kind of underdocumented-in-English works the archive is built for.

**Estimated gap:** 10-15 titles
**Era:** 1972-1985

### 2. TATSUNOKO PRODUCTION — 1970s Originals
**Why:** The original Gatchaman, Speed Racer, Tekkaman, and Time Bokan series are foundational. The archive has adaptations/remakes but not the originals.

**Estimated gap:** 8-12 titles
**Era:** 1967-1983

### 3. BONES — Modern Sci-Fi Catalog
**Why:** Eureka Seven, Darker than Black, Space Dandy, and Xam'd are significant sci-fi works from a major modern studio. Currently only 3-6 entries.

**Estimated gap:** 6-10 titles
**Era:** 2003-present

### 4. EVANGELION REBUILD FILMS (Studio Khara)
**Why:** Four films (1.0 through 3.0+1.0) that may be absent as individual entries despite the original Eva being well-covered. The culmination of one of the most important anime franchises.

**Estimated gap:** 4 titles
**Era:** 2007-2021

### 5. SUNRISE — VOTOMS + Ideon + Brave Series
**Why:** The main VOTOMS TV series and Space Runaway Ideon are essential Tomino/Sunrise works. The Brave series (GaoGaiGar especially) filled the super robot gap between classic and modern.

**Estimated gap:** 8-12 titles
**Era:** 1980-2000

### 6. TMS ENTERTAINMENT — Space Cobra + God Mars
**Why:** Small gap but historically important. Space Cobra is a beloved space opera. Need to also verify if Akira is in the DB (TMS production).

**Estimated gap:** 3-5 titles
