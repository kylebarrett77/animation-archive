## Architecture: How the Archive Works

The archive is building toward comprehensive coverage of all underdocumented global animation, approached **sector by sector**. Each thematic sector gets worked through systematically — studio by studio, catalog by catalog — before moving to the next.

**Current active sector: Science Fiction.** Based on genre/keyword analysis of a 449-entry sample (5 views), ~54% of visible entries carry sci-fi genre tags (Mecha, Space Opera, Cyberpunk, Dystopia, etc.) or sci-fi keywords (Aliens, Space, Robots, AI, Earth Future, etc.). Extrapolated across 1,793 films, the sci-fi sector likely accounts for ~900-1,000 entries.

The remaining ~800 entries are foundational — comprehensive studio catalogs (Soyuzmultfilm, Fleischer, etc.), historical milestones, and entries that will serve as backbone when future thematic sectors are activated.
